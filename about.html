    • Hi, I'm Mars.

    I am a programmer, musician, and video editor, born and raised in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. I started programming when I was around ~8 years old, beginning with Lua(u) in Roblox.

    By 13, I had tinkered with many languages, such as Python, C++, C#, HTML/CSS/JS, and more. I enjoyed every language and learned them to varying levels.

    I started writing and producing music at 13, starting with electronic singles under mars.mp3. This pseudonym eventually became my miscellaneous creative outlet. At 15, I started my death metal / hardcore band Decaying Face, in which I play lead guitar and do vocals.

    I started my YouTube channel at 7 years old, making short form vlog videos alongside occassional gameplay content. By 13, I started making high-produced commentary content on varying topics.
